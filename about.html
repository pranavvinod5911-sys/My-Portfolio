About Me

Daily Life (more or less)

My day usually starts with a plan… and ends with me doing something completely different.

School, assignments, random ideas, and then somehow I end up working on a project at night like I have a deadline that doesn’t even exist.

Most of my productive work happens at the most questionable hours — which explains a lot.

How I Work

I don’t really follow a perfect system. I just start building something, break it, fix it, improve it, and repeat until it looks like I knew what I was doing from the beginning.

I like figuring things out myself first, even if it takes longer. Makes it way more satisfying when it finally works.

What I Like

• Breaking Bad
• Clean and simple design
• Projects that actually feel real
• That one moment when everything finally works

What I Don’t Like:

• Bugs that make no sense
• Code working yesterday but not today
• McDonald's (exept for the fries)
• Waking up early (let’s be honest)

Personality (probably accurate)

I’m someone who likes building more than talking about building. I’ll spend hours working on something small just to make it feel right.

I’m also the type to randomly get an idea and immediately start working on it, even if I have other things to do.

Current Focus

Right now, I’m focused on getting better at web development, improving how things look and feel, and building projects that are actually useful.
